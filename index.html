Privacy Policy

Photo Report (package com.jk.photoreport) is made by JK Information Technology. This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera to take photos. Each photo is stamped on your phone and saved in your gallery, in Pictures/Photo Report. Photos are not uploaded to JK Information Technology.

Location

If you allow location, the app reads your position to stamp the place name, latitude, and longitude on the photo. The place name is looked up through your phone’s location service, which may send the coordinates to that service. The coordinates are also sent to Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) to get the current weather for that stamp. JK Information Technology does not receive or store your location.

Information you type

Company name, staff name, logo, and stamp settings are saved only on your phone.

What we do not do

The app has no account, no ads, and no analytics. It does not sell your information.
